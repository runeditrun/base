# Edition first-release acceptance guidance

Keep one compact `ACCEPTANCE.md` in the edition source, alongside CONTRACT and
HANDOVER. The edition owns this decision; a family or Base verification command
must not infer it from a successful build or publication.

Record the edition's owner purpose and complete minimum journey, including its
failure behavior. Link existing contract ID families instead of duplicating every
clause. Record unmet first-release obligations separately from preserved wider
requirements and external unknowns. Keep owner-required minima, privacy, authority,
truthful outcomes and applicable operating guarantees in force. A narrower profile
is not a waiver of a contract or its explicit full-release conditions; unresolved
scope conflicts remain visible until deliberately settled. Never delete original
promises or mark uncovered clauses satisfied to make a gate green.

Include exact candidate and dated evidence references. Distinguish executed gates,
failed/skipped/unexecuted coverage, publication, deployment, provider acceptance,
confirmed business effects and mailbox delivery. Historical pilot evidence is not
a fresh candidate verification. Preserve fixture, temporary-session and test-mode
limitations. A successful `verify` reports the checks it ran; completion additionally
requires evidence for the minimum outcome and applicable guarantees, resolution of
scope conflicts, and honest disposition of remaining requirements.

Suggested compact structure: purpose and minimum outcome; unmet release obligations;
preserved wider requirements; evidence and decision rule. No second per-clause
registry is needed when the contract and its existing reporter already hold it.
